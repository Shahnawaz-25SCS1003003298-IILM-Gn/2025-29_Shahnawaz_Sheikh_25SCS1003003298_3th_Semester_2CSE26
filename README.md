## 📌 Project Overview

Coral bleaching is a major threat to coral reef ecosystems and is strongly influenced by environmental stress, particularly abnormal sea-surface temperatures.

The **Coral Reef Bleaching Prediction Dashboard** brings together historical coral reef information, environmental parameters, machine-learning-based risk prediction, geographic visualization, and climate-scenario analysis in a single interface.

The dashboard helps users understand coral reef conditions and converts complex environmental information into easier-to-understand risk information.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze historical coral bleaching trends.
- Visualize coral reef survey locations on an interactive map.
- Analyze environmental factors associated with bleaching.
- Predict coral bleaching risk using environmental parameters.
- Classify bleaching risk as **Low, Medium, or High**.
- Display prediction confidence and class probabilities.
- Provide an assessment and recommended action.
- Simulate future reef conditions under different climate scenarios.

---

## 🖥️ Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes:

- Historical bleaching statistics
- Yearly bleaching trends
- Ocean-wise bleaching analysis
- Country-wise bleaching analysis
- Temperature vs. bleaching analysis
- SSTA vs. bleaching analysis
- Interactive global reef survey map
- Bleaching Risk Predictor
- 12 adjustable environmental parameters
- Prediction confidence and probabilities
- AI-based assessment
- Recommended conservation action
- 10-year climate scenario simulation
- Community feedback module
